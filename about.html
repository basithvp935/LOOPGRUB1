WHO WE ARE

LoopGrub Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a technology-driven food resource recovery company dedicated to transforming approved food materials into valuable products through innovative and sustainable processing solutions.

We partner with food manufacturers, bakeries, hotels, retailers, supermarkets, and commercial establishments to recover value from food waste, protecting the environment, and supporting the circular economy.

Our mission extends beyond waste management. We focus on maximizing resource recovery through advanced technology, operational excellence, and responsible processing.

OUR MISSION

Maximizing Resource Recovery for a Greener Tomorrow

  • Recover value from food waste responsibly.
  • Develop innovative and efficient processing technologies.
  • Support sustainable industries and communities.
  • Protect natural resources and reduce environmental impact.
  • Deliver safe, efficient and reliable recovery solutions.
  • Create long-term value for our customers, partners and society.

Foundation

Established with a clear vision to address food waste and create sustainable solutions. Our journey began with a commitment to transforming linear waste models into circular economies, focusing on reducing landfill impact while recovering valuable resources.

Technology Development

Invested in research and developed advanced machines and processes for efficient recovery. We've built proprietary biological and mechanical systems that rapidly process organic matter with zero emissions, setting a new industry standard.

Innovation & Growth

Expanded our capabilities and built a strong foundation in food resource recovery. Our team scaled operations to handle multiple tons of waste daily, integrating IoT sensors and AI-driven analytics to optimize the breakdown process.

Industry Partnerships

Partnered with leading businesses across industries to recover value and reduce waste. From major hospitality chains to local municipalities, we've created a collaborative network dedicated to closed-loop sustainability.

Sustainable Impact

Continuously working towards a cleaner planet and a circular economy. We've successfully diverted thousands of tons of organic matter from landfills, converting it into high-grade compost and renewable bio-energy.

Future Forward

Committed to innovation, responsibility and creating a better future for generations. Our roadmap includes expanding globally and introducing next-gen biotechnologies to completely eliminate commercial food waste.

OUR COMMITMENT

At LoopGrub, we combine technology, responsibility and passion to turn waste into resources. Together with our partners, we are building a cleaner, greener and more sustainable tomorrow.
