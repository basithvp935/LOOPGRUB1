OUR TECHNOLOGY PROCESS

A seamless integration of machines, systems and expertise.

1

RECEIVING

Approved food materials are received and inspected for quality and compliance.

2

AUTOMATED UNPACKING
& PRE-SORTING

Machines remove packaging and perform pre-sorting to ensure clean input material.

3

ADVANCED SORTING
SYSTEM

Multi-stage sorting removes contaminants and ensures uniform material quality.

4

DRYING AND BLENDING TECHNOLOGY

Controlled drum drying reduces moisture efficiently while preserving nutrients.

5

INGREDIENT MIXING

Dried material is blended with essential ingredients to achieve consistent nutritional value.

6

STORAGE SYSTEM

Dried material is stored in hygienic silos with moisture and temperature control.

7

OUTPUT PRODUCTS

Processed material is converted into valuable resources for various uses.
